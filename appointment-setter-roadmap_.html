Appointment Setting Business
6-Month Roadmap
Fitness coaches · High ticket · First $1K online
Month 1 of 6 0%
01 Foundation
Build your foundation
Learn the craft. Build the tools. Get ready to operate.
💰 Target: $0 — prep month
Objectives
1
Master the model
›
  • Watch 10+ YouTube videos on appointment setting — search "appointment setter fitness coach"
  • Follow 5 active setters on Instagram and study their content daily
  • Understand commission structures: $150–$200 per booked showed call
  • Learn what a "showed call" means and why it's the standard payment unit
2
Study your niche
›
  • Spend 1 hour per day in Meta Ad Library — search "weight loss coach" and "fitness coach"
  • Save 50 fitness coaches running ads — screenshot their ads and landing pages
  • Understand their offers: what do $1,500–$5,000 fitness programs include?
  • Learn their customer's pain points, objections, and the language they use
3
Write your core scripts
›
  • Write your client outreach DM — pitching yourself to coaches
  • Write a lead qualification script: 5–7 questions to vet a prospect
  • Write booking confirmation + 24hr reminder messages
  • Write a re-engagement opener for cold or dead leads
4
Set up your tech stack
›
  • Create a Google Workspace account — professional email address
  • Build a Notion tracker: Name, Platform, Status, Outreach Date, Follow-up Date, Notes
  • Create a free Calendly link to book discovery calls with potential clients
  • Set up your Instagram brand page: handle, bio, and first 3 posts live
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 3x per week — document your setup process in real time
  • Share what you're learning: what appointment setting is, how setters get paid
  • Show your workspace, Notion tracker being built, scripts being written
  • Goal: 12 posts total. Target 50–100 followers
Criteria
Exit checklist
  • ✓ All 4 scripts written
  • ✓ Notion CRM built
  • ✓ 50 coaches saved
  • ✓ Instagram live, 12 posts
  • ✓ Can explain model clearly
Month 2 entry
  • → Scripts saved and ready
  • → 50 prospects in Notion
  • → Professional email live
  • → Confident pitching yourself
02 Outreach
Land your first client
Volume outreach. Follow up relentlessly. Close your first deal.
💰 Target: $0–$750
Objectives
1
Run daily outreach
›
  • Send 10–15 DMs or emails per day to fitness coaches from your saved list
  • Lead with their pain: "You're running ads — are all your leads being followed up with?"
  • Keep it short: 3–4 sentences max, no walls of text
  • Track every outreach in Notion: date sent, platform, response, follow-up needed
2
Follow up on every prospect
›
  • Follow up 3–5 times before marking anyone as dead — most deals close on follow-up 3 or 4
  • Space follow-ups: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, Day 21
  • Each follow-up should add value or a new angle — not just "checking in"
  • Add 10 new prospects per day to replace anyone who goes permanently cold
3
Offer a 2-week free trial
›
  • When you get a response, offer to work free for 14 days — removes all risk for them
  • Your goal during the trial: book 5–10 calls and convert to a paid deal
  • On the discovery call, ask: "Walk me through how your leads come in and where they go"
  • Get access to their DMs, email, or CRM — learn their system before you touch anything
4
Handle rejection without stopping
›
  • Expect to pitch 50–100 coaches before landing 1 — this is normal, not failure
  • Every "no" is data — keep a note of the objection and look for patterns
  • If you get the same objection 5+ times, rewrite that part of your pitch
  • Track your conversion rate: responses ÷ outreach sent
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 3–4x per week — document your outreach process in real time
  • Share rejection stories honestly: "I got 12 no's this week, here's what I learned"
  • Educate: "How I find fitness coaches to work with" / "What I say in my first DM"
  • Show your Notion tracker and message templates (blurred if needed)
  • Goal: 200–300 followers by end of month
Criteria
Exit checklist
  • ✓ 150–200 coaches pitched
  • ✓ 1 client on free trial
  • ✓ Real leads being worked
  • ✓ 200+ Instagram followers
Month 3 entry
  • → 1 active client
  • → Access to their leads
  • → Daily outreach habit locked
03 Execution
Deliver results and get paid
Execute for your client. Hit your first $1,000. Get proof.
💰 Target: $1,000–$2,250
Objectives
1
Work leads every single day
›
  • Log in to your client's DMs, email, or CRM every morning — respond within the hour
  • Work every lead source: DMs, story replies, email list, old applications, "not now" people
  • Send 20–30 follow-up messages per day across all their lead sources
  • Speed is your edge — respond before anyone else does
2
Qualify and book calls
›
  • Run every interested lead through your qualification script before booking
  • Only book leads who are serious — protect your client's calendar
  • Send booking link + confirmation immediately after qualifying
  • Send a reminder 24 hours before and 1 hour before every call
  • Target: 8–15 booked showed calls this month
3
Report to your client weekly
›
  • Send a weekly report: conversations opened, responses, bookings, shows, no-shows
  • Flag patterns: "Most leads go cold after 3 days — should I be more aggressive?"
  • Be proactive — don't wait for them to ask how it's going
  • Position yourself as an asset, not a vendor
4
Convert trial to paid, get testimonial
›
  • At day 14, present results: X leads contacted, X booked, X showed
  • Propose paid deal: $500–$800/mo retainer + $150/booked showed call
  • Ask for a written testimonial and a referral to another coach they know
  • If they don't convert, use the data and testimonial to land your next client faster
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 4x per week — you now have real stories to tell
  • "I booked X calls for my client this week — here's how" (blur client name)
  • Share a qualification script breakdown, a re-engagement message that worked
  • Post your first income milestone when you hit it — people love real numbers
  • Goal: 400–500 followers by end of month
Criteria
Exit checklist
  • ✓ First $1,000 earned
  • ✓ 1 paid client on retainer
  • ✓ 1 written testimonial
  • ✓ Weekly reporting system set
  • ✓ 400+ Instagram followers
Month 4 entry
  • → $1,000+ collected
  • → 1 active paying client
  • → Testimonial in hand
  • → Outreach pipeline running
04 Optimize
Optimize and add a second client
Double your income. Tighten your process. Build a case study.
💰 Target: $2,000–$4,000
Objectives
1
Refine scripts with real data
›
  • Review every conversation from Month 3 — what language made leads respond? What killed them?
  • Rewrite your re-engagement opener and qualification script based on actual results
  • Build a swipe file of your best-performing messages — these become your templates
  • Track your show rate — aim for 70%+. If lower, improve your reminder sequence
2
Land a second client
›
  • Continue outreach to 5–10 coaches per day even while delivering for Client 1
  • Use Month 3 results as your pitch: "I booked X calls for a fitness coach last month"
  • Lead with your testimonial — social proof closes faster than any pitch
  • Skip the free trial this time — charge from day one ($150–$200/showed call)
3
Build your case study
›
  • Document Client 1 results: leads worked, calls booked, show rate, revenue recovered
  • Turn this into a simple PDF or Instagram carousel you can share with prospects
  • This becomes your #1 sales tool for every future client conversation
4
Set up legal and financial basics
›
  • Register an LLC in your state ($50–$200 on your state's website)
  • Draft a contractor agreement: commission rate, payment terms, 30-day termination clause
  • Set up Wave (free) for invoicing — invoice clients on the 1st of each month
  • Open a separate business bank account
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 4x per week — you now have a case study and real results to share
  • "How I went from 0 to my first $1,000 as an appointment setter" — post this
  • Share process breakdowns, scripts, and your daily workflow
  • Start engaging with other setter accounts — comment, collaborate, be visible
  • Goal: 600–700 followers by end of month
Criteria
Exit checklist
  • ✓ 2 active paying clients
  • ✓ Case study documented
  • ✓ LLC registered
  • ✓ $2,000+ earned this month
  • ✓ 600+ Instagram followers
Month 5 entry
  • → 2 clients actively running
  • → Case study ready to share
  • → Legal and invoicing in place
  • → Systems handle 2 clients
05 Scale
Scale to 3 clients
Raise rates. Add volume. Let inbound start working for you.
💰 Target: $3,500–$6,000
Objectives
1
Add a third client
›
  • You now have 2 case studies and 2 testimonials — your pitch is significantly stronger
  • Outreach 5x per day (reduced — you're spending more time delivering now)
  • Look for inbound from Instagram — DMs and comments asking how to hire you
  • Charge higher for Client 3: $200–$250/showed call or $1,000–$1,500/mo retainer
2
Renegotiate with existing clients
›
  • At Month 5, you've proven ROI — use this to renegotiate rates upward
  • Present results: "I've booked X calls, recovered approximately $X in revenue for you"
  • Ask for a rate increase of $25–$50 per showed call or add a monthly retainer
  • Clients who see results will happily pay more — ask confidently
3
Systematize your workflow
›
  • Build a daily checklist for each client: morning check-in, lead response block, reporting
  • Template every message you send repeatedly — save them in Notion for quick access
  • Time-block your day: Client 1 (8–9am), Client 2 (9–10am), Client 3 (10–11am), outreach (11am–12pm)
  • If volume gets unmanageable, research hiring a VA setter under you
4
Build your Instagram flywheel
›
  • 3+ months of real results — your content is increasingly powerful
  • Post income milestones, behind-the-scenes, client wins (anonymized)
  • Add a CTA to every post: "DM me 'setter' if you want to learn how to do this"
  • Reply to every comment and DM — community builds faster than follower count
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 4–5x per week — content should feel urgent and real
  • "I now manage 3 clients as an appointment setter — here's my daily schedule"
  • Income report posts: show your monthly total — builds credibility fast
  • Begin an educational series: "Appointment setting 101" multi-part content
  • Goal: 800–900 followers by end of month
Criteria
Exit checklist
  • ✓ 3 active paying clients
  • ✓ Rates raised on 1+ client
  • ✓ Workflow systematized
  • ✓ $3,500+ earned this month
  • ✓ 800+ Instagram followers
Month 6 entry
  • → 3 clients running smoothly
  • → Systems handling volume
  • → Inbound from Instagram starting
06 Establish
Establish and expand
Hit $5K+/mo. Hit 1,000 followers. Build what lasts.
💰 Target: $5,000–$8,000
Objectives
1
Raise rates across the board
›
  • You have 5 months of results — you are no longer a beginner, price accordingly
  • Move to $250–$300/showed call or $1,500+/mo retainer + commission
  • If any client pushes back, show them your numbers — results justify the rate
  • Drop any client that isn't profitable for the rate of work they require
2
Introduce the Muslim business niche
›
  • With skills sharpened, begin pitching 3–5 Muslim coaches or consultants per week
  • Transfer your fitness results to prove competence in a new niche
  • Position yourself uniquely: "I understand your audience and your values"
  • This becomes your second lane and long-term brand differentiation
3
Consider hiring a VA setter
›
  • If you're at capacity (3 clients, 30+ hrs/week), document your full process first
  • Hire a VA on Fiverr or Upwork for $5–$10/hr to handle volume outreach
  • You manage strategy, reporting, and client relationships — VA handles message volume
  • This is how a one-person setter becomes an agency
4
Hit 1,000 Instagram followers
›
  • Sprint the final push — post 5–6x per week this month
  • Engage aggressively: reply to every comment, DM everyone who follows you
  • Post your 6-month income and journey recap — your highest-performing post of the journey
  • Announce what's next — builds anticipation and locks in follower loyalty
Instagram this month
📱 Content focus
  • Post 5–6x per week — final push to 1,000
  • "6 months. Here's everything that happened." — full journey recap post
  • Share your income from each month in a chart or carousel
  • "How to become an appointment setter in 2025" — most shareable educational post yet
  • Goal: 1,000 followers by end of month
Criteria
